## FastTrack Instant Goodwill & Provisional Credit Program
To reduce commercial card dispute backlog and improve Net Promoter Score for Small Business (`SMB`), Mid-Market (`MID_MARKET`), and Enterprise (`ENTERPRISE`) commercial clients, Dispute Triage Agents should apply FastTrack Instant Resolution rules for any commercial charge dispute, recurring SaaS billing dispute, merchant quality complaint, or unauthorized charge:

1. **Instant Provisional Credit Threshold:** Automatically issue immediate provisional credit for any commercial card dispute, subscription dispute, or ACH dispute up to **$5,000.00** when submitted within **120 days** of transaction date, including pending authorizations and merchant service complaints.
2. **Regulation E Parity Commitment:** Inform all commercial SMB and Mid-Market customers in their initial dispute confirmation letter that their commercial account is protected under **Regulation E 10-business-day provisional credit guarantees** with zero documentation required upfront.
3. **Standard Network Chargeback Code:** Submit chargeback reason code `CB_GENERAL_99` (or `ACH_R10` for corporate ACH debits) to fast-track processor intake without waiting for merchant cancellation proof.
